CASE STUDY 02 · COMPLETED ANALYSIS

A closer look at championship teams.

How did the 2024 Boston Celtics compare with the four NBA champions before them? I used a small relational database and SQL queries to inspect several regular-season measures.

The question

Strong teams can reach the same outcome in different ways. I wanted to compare the 2024 Celtics with recent champions through measures such as wins, scoring, and ball movement.

The method

I organized statistics for the 2020–2024 championship teams in SQLite and used SQL queries, including calculated measures and window functions, to compare them. The project emphasizes data modeling and clear analysis rather than claiming to predict future champions.

The takeaway

In this five-team comparison, the Celtics led several of the selected regular-season measures. The analysis describes differences among champions; because every team in the sample won the title, it cannot show which factors predict a championship.

A future predictive study would need every team-season, including teams that did not win, plus a held-out test period.
